\noindent\textbf{Tier naming.} Throughout the main paper and this document, $t_1$ = PDP-derived device, processing and composition features; $t_2$ = $t_1$ + curated material-property descriptors; $t_3$ = $t_2$ + the 36 LLM-derived recipe flags. An earlier, superseded 44-predictor literature representation (the ``earlier literature block'') is \emph{not} $t_3$; where its results are shown they are labelled ``$t_2$ + earlier lit.\ block''. In the underlying run files and result tables of the repository this block carries the tier name \texttt{t3}; this document relabels it and never mixes its numbers with the recipe-flag results.\par

\section*{Material-descriptor implementation notes}
\noindent\textbf{Perovskite geometry descriptors.} Fixed effective radii (pm): A-site FA 253, MA 217, Cs 188, Rb 172; B-site Pb 119, Sn 110, Bi 103; X-site I 220, Br 196, Cl 181, F 133. Site radius = fraction-weighted mean over the listed species of that site, divided by the sum of their fractions; $\mu=r_B/r_X$, $t=(r_A+r_X)/[\sqrt2(r_B+r_X)]$, $r_A/r_X$, $\tau=r_X/r_B-(1-(r_A/r_B)\ln(r_A/r_B))$ (script \texttt{opus/evaluate\_tolerance\_factor.py}; the stored octahedral and tolerance factors were reproduced to floating-point precision from the ion-fraction columns). \textbf{Layer averages.} \texttt{*\_avg\_radius}, \texttt{*\_avg\_atomic\_mass} and \texttt{*\_en\_avg} are atom-count-weighted means of covalent radii (pm), atomic masses and Pauling electronegativities over the elements of a hand-entered formula (script \texttt{opus/fill\_atomic\_data.py}); \texttt{min}/\texttt{max} are the extremes over the elements. For MAPbI$_3$ (C$_1$H$_6$N$_1$Pb$_1$I$_3$) the average radius is $(77+6\cdot31+71+146+3\cdot139)/12=74.75$ pm, matching the stored value.\par
